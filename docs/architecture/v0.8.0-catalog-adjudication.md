## The criterion

**A bridge is an edge whose endpoint quantities differ in at least one
regime attribute; a law is an edge whose endpoints share all stated
regime attributes.** For graph edges this is `regimesDiffer`
(`src/composition/quantity.ts`); for catalog entries the
`bridges: [a, b]` tuple is the proxy, with the negative-catalog
registry overlaying it (an id in `rejected.ts` is `'not-a-bridge'`
regardless of tuple — the historical `['unknown','unknown']` marker
cannot distinguish "rejected" from "never adjudicated" on its own).

## What this does NOT do

- Rejected entries are NOT removed from `BRIDGE_EQUATIONS` — their
  formulas and AST encodings remain valid physics; rejection is an
  adjudication overlay (P-4), not deletion.
- No status (`established`/`speculative`/…) changed — membership and
  credibility are independent axes.
- The three contested entries are deliberately left open with the
  reason recorded; they are the catalog's standing physicist-review
  tasks, not backlog debt.
