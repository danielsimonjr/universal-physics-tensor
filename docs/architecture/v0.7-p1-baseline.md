## Task 0.1 — Axis-value census

Cross-checked against `src/core/types.ts` HEAD (lines 10-48):

| Axis | Count | Members |
|---|---:|---|
| `PhysicalScale` | 4 | `quantum`, `mesoscopic`, `classical`, `cosmological` |
| `Force` | 5 | `gravitational`, `electromagnetic`, `weak`, `strong`, `emergent` |
| `Symmetry` | 5 | `poincare`, `gauge`, `conformal`, `susy`, `emergent` |
| `InformationMeasure` | 4 | `vonNeumann`, `shannon`, `kolmogorov`, `quantumDiscord` |

**Total enumerable axis values: 18** (4 + 5 + 5 + 4).

Plus two integer axes per `TensorConfig`:
  - `dimension` (positive integer, undeclared range — historically used as 3 or 4)
  - `topology` (signed integer, undeclared range — used as 0, ±1)

Per Decision #10 + the P2 `unpopulatedNeighborhoods()` precedent,
the integer axes are wildcards in `Axes` registry semantics. The
`Axes` namespace ships ONLY `Axes.scale`, `Axes.force`,
`Axes.symmetry`, `Axes.information` — 4 namespaces, 18 stable
`UniversalIndex` references at module load.

**Design claim cross-check:** Decision #4 said "the proposals doc §2.4
figures (4 scales, 5 forces, 4+placeholder symmetries, 4 information
measures) may be stale." Actual is **5 symmetries (no placeholder)**.
Mild discrepancy from the design's hedge: `emergent` is a legitimate
member, not a placeholder. Phase 1 `Axes.symmetry` ships all 5.
