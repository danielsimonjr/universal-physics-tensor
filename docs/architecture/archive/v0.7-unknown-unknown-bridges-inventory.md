## How to use this doc

For each of the 26 entries below, the editorial task is:

1. Read the entry in `src/bridges/index.ts` (the `name`, `notes`,
   `references`, and `formula_latex` together describe what the
   bridge IS).
2. Decide: which two regimes does this bridge connect? Candidate
   regime names come from existing `PhysicalScale` /
   `Force` / `Symmetry` / `InformationMeasure` unions or from the
   freeform labels already in other entries
   (`microscale`, `emergent`, `information`, `physical`,
   `consciousness`, `gravity`, `dark-sector`,
   `condensed-matter`, `holography`, `field-A`/`field-B`,
   `Newtonian gravity`, `general relativity`, ...).
3. **Or**: acknowledge per audit §3 that "the entry is 'an
   important equation in regime X' rather than a bridge" and
   reclassify (this is the bridge-incoherence finding — the
   equation is real physics but doesn't span two regimes).
4. Apply: edit the `bridges: [\`unknown\`, \`unknown\`]` line to
   the chosen pair; update `notes:` with the rationale.

The cleanest editorial discipline: name **only** when you can
defend the pair with one sentence in `notes:`. Leave as `unknown,
unknown` when the answer is "this is an important equation, not
a bridge" — that's a category-clarity signal, not a defect.

## Category of editorial outcomes

For each of the 26 entries, the editorial choice is one of:

| Outcome | When | How to record |
|---|---|---|
| **(A) Name the bridge** | The equation genuinely connects two distinct regimes | Edit `bridges: [\`X\`, \`Y\`]` + add `notes:` justification |
| **(B) Reclassify as not-a-bridge** | The equation is real physics but lives in ONE regime | Move to a "single-regime laws" sub-catalog (out of v0.7 scope — would need a parallel `LAW_EQUATIONS` array) OR add `notes:` field "Not a bridge — single-regime equation in [X]" and leave `bridges:` as `unknown,unknown` to signal the category mismatch |
| **(C) Defer** | Genuine uncertainty about which regimes | Leave as `unknown,unknown`; add `notes:` line saying "Bridge regime pair UNRESOLVED — editorial review needed" |

The audit's recommendation is to drive the count of (A) up by
applying (A) where defensible AND (B) or (C) where not — the
*signal* matters more than the absolute count.
