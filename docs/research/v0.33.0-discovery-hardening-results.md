# Discovery-Hardening Program — Results (v0.31.0 – v0.33.0)

**Date:** 2026-07-03 · **Scope:** the honest end-to-end results of the
four-phase discovery-hardening program (adjudication ledger → axis falsifier →
evidence channels → consequence propagation). Every number below regenerates
from a `upt` command or a pinned test named in-line; nothing here is asserted
without a reproducer.

## Premise

UPT proposes a rank-6 tensor **Π** whose off-diagonal "bridge" entries connect
physical regimes, and a discovery funnel that proposes cross-cluster quantity
identifications (a ≡ b) and vets them. The program's question is blunt:
**when the machinery proposes that two quantities from different physics
clusters are the same, is it ever right?** The four phases added successively
stronger falsifiers to answer it honestly rather than optimistically.

## The funnel and its falsifiers

`upt discover` proposes cross-cluster candidate pairs that share an SI
dimension and vets each through, in order of strength:

1. **Dimensional** — do a and b share a dimension? (weak: a coincidence-heavy
   prior; same dimension ≠ same quantity).
2. **Magnitude** — are their representative values within a bounded
   orders-of-magnitude window? (`magnitude-clash`).
3. **Axis compatibility** (Phase 2) — an identification means a ≡ b under two
   names, so their stated `scale`/`force` regime attributes must agree; a
   genuine disagreement falsifies the pair (`axis-clash`). The first
   mechanism-*proxy* falsifier.
4. **Adjudication ledger** (Phase 1) — recorded human verdicts fold
   already-reviewed candidates out of the worklist (review memory).
5. **Consequence propagation** (Phase 4) — substitute a ≡ b through the
   symbolic composition, derive its monomial algebraic consequence, and check
   whether that consequence re-derives a canonical equation (`entailed`),
   states a new non-contradicted relation (`novel-consequence`), or is
   algebraically too thin to classify (`inconclusive`). The first falsifier
   that reads an identification's *logical consequence*.

## Negative results — the honest core

**The framework rejects, and correctly so.** Across every review round the
funnel has surfaced **zero** identifications that a human adjudicator marked
`genuine`.

- **Adjudication ledger: 0 of 8 genuine.** The eight human-reviewed candidates
  (`src/composition/adjudication.ts` `ADJUDICATIONS`; provenance
  `docs/research/proposed-equations-adjudication.md`,
  `orphan-connector-adjudication.md`) resolve as **7 `decoy` + 1 `entailed`,
  0 `genuine`** — every reviewed cross-cluster coincidence was either a
  dimensional accident or a restatement of known physics.
- **Canonical self-consistency: `contradictory = 0`.** On the standard-physics
  L-layer alone, the funnel finds no self-contradiction (calibration benchmark
  `tests/composition/discovery-calibration.test.ts`, catalog line
  `132 / 7 promising / 35 inert / 20 magnitude-clash / 0 contradictory /
  70 axis-clash`). The framework does not contradict textbook physics.
- **Axis gate: 70 catalog pairs falsified as cross-regime coincidences**, and
  the 5 that had read `promising` before the gate are named and pinned
  (`CATALOG_PROMISING_TO_AXIS_CLASH`): `boundary-length ≟ schwarzschild-radius`,
  `coarsening-length ≟ schwarzschild-radius`, `coarsening-length ≟
  thermal-wavelength`, `grw-localization-rate ≟ hubble-rate`,
  `grw-localization-rate ≟ mutation-rate`. Each is dimensionally exact and
  physically meaningless — a black-hole radius is not a condensed-matter
  coarsening length. The axis gate cut catalog `promising` 12 → 7.
- **Consequence propagation: 0 `entailed`, 4 `novel-consequence`** (Phase-4
  Task-0 measurement, reproduced by `tests/composition/consequence.test.ts`).
  No current `promising` candidate re-derives a known canonical equation —
  meaning none is a disguised textbook identity — and the four that carry a
  derivable consequence state relations with no canonical match. Every
  promising candidate is therefore genuinely-new-or-algebraically-thin, none a
  restatement.

**Interpretation.** The rank-6 tensor's connectivity is *aspirational*. `upt
map` finds the catalog graph sparse (23 connected components, 20 isolated
bridges — a hub-and-spoke star, not a connected manifold; see
`docs/research/Catalog-Linkage-Map.md`), and the falsifier stack shows that
almost all cross-cluster "links" a dimensional search proposes are
coincidences. This is the program's central honest finding: **UPT is a
rigorous coincidence-rejector.** A discovery framework that mostly says "no,
that's a coincidence" — and can prove why — is more scientifically valuable
than one that manufactures false unifications.

### A rejected discovery mode — cross-cluster Buckingham-π (a negative *methodological* result)

A candidate fifth falsifier/discovery-mode — enumerate dimensionless π-groups
across cluster-boundary quantities and surface those whose numeric value lands
near a recognizable constant (1, 2π, ln 2, α) — was **tested and rejected**
before implementation. A bounded Task-0 probe measured 16,979 single-invariant
π-groups with a 4.3 % background rate of landing near one of an 8-constant table
at a fixed tolerance. Expected pure-chance "hits" = 16,979 × 0.043 ≈ **730** — a
catastrophic multiple-comparisons problem in which every surfaced hit is almost
certainly noise. Compounding this, a π-group's numeric value is an artifact of
the *chosen* representative scale for each quantity (dimensional analysis
returns form, not value), so a "hit" is manufacturable, not physical.
Adversarial design review returned RED unanimously; the mode was **not built**.
The honest conclusion is consistent with everything above: **the frontier
bridges are isolated by physics**, and a coincidence-generator would contradict
the framework's coincidence-rejector character. (Design + full rationale:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-03-discovery-hardening-phase4-unitB-design.md`.)

## Positive results — the evidence spine

Where UPT's *established* bridges encode real physics, they confront real data
within error. `upt confront` runs 5 committed confrontations
(`DATA_CONFRONTED_IDS = {23, 36, 37, 48, 52}`):

| bridge | confrontation | result |
|---|---|---|
| BE-52 | GR perihelion precession vs Mercury (Clemence 1947, 43.11 ± 0.45 ″/cy) | **within 1σ** (predicted 42.99, residual ≈ 0.26σ) |
| BE-37 | GR Shapiro delay / PPN γ vs Cassini (Bertotti-Iess-Tortora 2003, γ = 1+(2.1±2.3)×10⁻⁵) | **within 1σ** (residual ≈ 0.91σ) |
| BE-23 | Planckian dissipation α vs overdoped cuprates (Legros 2019) | α = O(1), consistent |
| BE-36 | GW speed vs GW170817 bound | encoded bound satisfied |
| BE-48 | GRW collapse rate vs LISA-Pathfinder CSL bound (Carlesso 2016) | not excluded (honest fail-to-exclude, GRW≠CSL caveat recorded) |

The two GR confrontations (Mercury, Cassini) are the framework's strongest
positive result: its established gravitational bridges reproduce the classic
tests of general relativity within experimental error, computed from the
bridge's own encoded formulation, not a textbook lookalike.

## What this means for a physicist

UPT is best understood as an **honest exploration instrument**, not a
unification claim. It:

- proposes cross-regime identifications and *falsifies almost all of them*,
  showing its work at each falsifier;
- confronts its established relations with real data and stays within error;
- refuses, by construction (the epistemic firewall), to let any machine
  verdict mutate the catalog — promotion to an established bridge requires a
  human review with citation.

The gaps are stated, not hidden: the empirical spine is thin (5 of 44 bridges
data-confronted), the connectivity is sparse, and the speculative bridges are
labeled speculative. Contributions that confront a speculative bridge with
data, or adjudicate a proposed identification, are the highest-value input
(see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## Reproducibility

Every number regenerates:

- funnel counts / falsifications: `node bin/upt.mjs discover --json` and
  `--source=canonical`; pinned by `tests/composition/discovery-calibration.test.ts`.
- confrontations: `node bin/upt.mjs confront --json`; pinned per-bridge in
  `tests/bridges/be*-confrontation*.test.ts`.
- consequence signals: `node bin/upt.mjs discover` (the `[consequence: …]`
  trailer); pinned by `tests/composition/consequence.test.ts`.
- coverage / connectivity: `node bin/upt.mjs coverage`, `upt map`, `upt connectors`.

The 0/8-genuine, 0-contradictory, 70-axis-clash, and 0-entailed figures are
standing regression gates — a future change that manufactures a false
`genuine` or resurfaces a falsified coincidence fails CI.
