## Findings already on record

1. **Quantity aliasing (mechanically surfaced).** Several candidates
   alias same-named quantities across operands (⚠ rows) — e.g.
   `be-42>>be-12` feeds ONE 'mass' input to both the black-hole mass
   and the particle mass, so its naive reading "thermal de Broglie
   wavelength of emitted quanta at T_H" is NOT what it computes. This
   is the quantity-identification workload the v0.8.0 design predicted,
   now surfaced by machine. Per-edge quantity namespacing is the v0.11
   design question; until then every ⚠ candidate needs a human to say
   whether the aliasing is physical (cf. ST-2, where it is) or not.
2. **Physically interesting non-aliased candidates** deserve first
   review attention (no ⚠ in the table).

## Review protocol

For each row: (a) is the junction identification physically justified?
(b) if ⚠, is the aliasing the intended reading? (c) does the composed
relation appear in the literature (cite), contradict it (cite), or
neither (the interesting case)? Reviewed rows get a verdict line added
beneath the table. No catalog status changes without the §XXVII-B
protocol.
