# Linkage Candidate Proposals — Using the Map to Find Missing Links

> **Provenance:** 2026-06-14 (branch
> `claude/bridge-equations-specs-review-4mfy38`). Uses the catalog linkage
> map (`Catalog-Linkage-Map.md`) to propose candidate identifications —
> the kind of cross-cluster link the Hawking-temperature ≡ temperature
> identification was, the one that fused the anchored core. Generated by
> `proposeLinkCandidates` (`src/composition/bridge-analysis.ts`);
> reproduce with `upt candidates`; pinned by
> `tests/composition/link-candidates.test.ts`.
>
> ⚠ **This is a REVIEW SURFACE, not a discovery.** Every candidate's only
> path is human adjudication (Part-VI §XXVII-B).

## Method

The map's clusters are connected by *shared quantities*. The gaps — two
quantities in **different** clusters with the **same dimension** but
different names — are candidate "missing links": if a physicist judged
them to be the same physical quantity, the clusters would join. We
generate every such pair (excluding pure dimensionless quantities, which
match too generically), and tag each with whether it touches the anchored
core and whether the two names share a word token (a weak "same physical
KIND" hint, e.g. both `*-mass`).

## The result is mostly noise — and that is the finding

This is the (A)-form-vs-(B)-discovery thesis of the whole inference suite,
quantified one last time. The funnel:

| filter | candidates |
|---|---|
| same (non-dimensionless) dimension, cross-cluster | **132** |
| …and touches the anchored core | 98 |
| …and same-kind (shared name token) | 36 |
| …and genuinely physically motivated (human judgment) | **≈ 3** |

**132 candidates from dimensional matching alone**, and even after two
mechanical filters (core-touching *and* same-kind), **34 of the surviving
36 are still rejectable on sight**:

- **Cross-kind coincidences** (62 of the 98 core-touching): a GRW collapse
  rate `≟` the Hubble rate; a superposition extent `≟` a Förster radius; a
  thermal de Broglie wavelength `≟` a coarsening length. Same dimension,
  unrelated physics.
- **Same-kind coincidences:** `decoherence-rate ≟ hubble-rate`,
  `carrier-density ≟ proton-density` (a cuprate's carriers are not the
  cosmos's protons), `defect-rest-mass ≟ swampland-tower-mass`.
- **Already-adjudicated-distinct:** `effective-mass ≟ mass`,
  `tunneling-mass ≟ defect-rest-mass`, `mass ≟ planck-mass` — these are
  pairs the catalog's authors *deliberately gave different names* because
  they are different objects despite identical dimensions. The candidate
  generator is, in effect, re-proposing links the project's quantity-naming
  discipline already rejected.

So the headline is the negative one, and it is the right one: **dimensional
matching cannot discover bridges** — it manufactures 132 plausible-looking
links, of which the overwhelming majority are coincidences or settled
distinctions. The human naming discipline is doing the real adjudication,
exactly as the epistemics note argued.

## The genuine few (for review)

A small number survive the filters *and* a physicist-style "are these the
same KIND of quantity in adjacent regimes?" test. These are proposed for
review, not asserted:

1. **`coarsening-length` ≟ `quantum-correlation-length` (and
   `reference-correlation-length`)** — `[length]`. The strongest
   candidate. BE-15 (Model-A coarsening) is currently **isolated**; BE-33
   (quantum criticality) and BE-34 (Kibble–Zurek) sit in the anchored
   core. The correlation length is *the* order parameter of critical
   dynamics, and classical coarsening ↔ quantum criticality are linked by
   **dynamic-scaling universality** — which is literally the Kibble–Zurek
   mechanism (BE-34). Identifying the coarsening length with the
   (quantum) correlation length would connect the isolated Model-A bridge
   to the criticality sub-cluster on a genuine physical basis. *A
   physicist should weigh whether this is one universality class or two.*
2. **`time` ≟ `microscopic-relaxation-time`** — `[time]`. The companion of
   (1): both are characteristic timescales of critical dynamics (BE-15 ↔
   BE-34). Same motivation.
3. **`landauer-erasure-energy` ≟ `inflation-hubble-energy` /
   `planck-mass-energy`** — `[energy]`. Weaker. Both are characteristic
   energies bridging information thermodynamics and cosmology/Planck
   physics — the *kind* of cross-regime link the catalog seeks, but these
   are different *scales*, so most likely a context-specific relation, not
   a global identification. Flagged, not recommended.

The honest expected outcome: (1)+(2) are worth a critical-phenomena
physicist's hour; (3) is probably a coincidence; everything else is.

## Takeaway

The map *can* propose candidates, and it surfaced one genuinely motivated
missing link (the critical-dynamics correlation length, connecting an
isolated bridge to the core). But the exercise's dominant result is the
132-to-3 funnel: **automated linkage proposal is a coincidence factory,
and its only legitimate output is a queue for human review** — which is
precisely the discipline UPT was built around. The generator is useful as
a *review surface*, not as a discovery engine.
