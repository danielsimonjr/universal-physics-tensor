> **Scope:** Post-v0.6.0 hygiene sprint. No new physics, no new public API,
> no behavior changes on the numerical or AST surface. Targets accumulated
> dead-code, unused exports, large-file factor-outs, dep-graph tooling
> false-positives, deferred bench harnesses, and stale documentation.

> **Inspiration & precedent:** v0.4.6 (25 tasks, 32 audit findings, the
> "minimize/simplify pass") and v0.5.1 (22 task commits across 8 phases,

> recurring hygiene-release pattern.

>   the vendored `tools/create-dependency-graph/`).

## Goal

Reduce the surface area of the codebase along five orthogonal axes while
preserving every public-API contract and every behavioral guarantee. The
sprint succeeds if:

1. The set of "unused exports" reported by `npm run docs:deps` drops from
   79 to ≤10, with each remaining item either explicitly `@public`-tagged
   (intentional surface) or accompanied by a one-line skip rationale in
   `unused-analysis.md`.
2. The set of "unused files" drops from 44 to ≤3 by teaching the
   dep-graph generator about dynamic catalog dispatch and `package.json`
   subpath exports. The remaining ≤3 are deliberately-retained
   reference modules with a one-line skip rationale.
3. `src/numerical/lowering.ts` and `src/dimensional/validator.ts` each
   drop below 900 LOC via factor-outs that pass Adam+Eve adversarial
   review.
4. Three bench harnesses (`bench/gl4-picard-alloc.bench.ts`,
   `bench/ricci-lowering.bench.ts`, `bench/pderiv-grid.bench.ts`) land
   with baseline numbers in `docs/architecture/benchmarks.md`.
5. `CLAUDE.md` no longer claims `.ruff_cache` exists; every "Current
   Version: v0.X.Y" or "as of v0.X.Y" reference is honest.
6. Suite stays ≥1693 (v0.6.0 baseline) — any net-new tests are net-add,
   not net-replace.

## Architectural Decisions (locked)

### Decision #1 — Balanced export-policy (per the maintainer's scoping choice)

Three buckets for the 79 reported "unused exports":

- **(a) GENUINELY INTERNAL** — declared `export` but used only inside its
  own module, or only by tests. Action: **delete the `export` keyword**
  (drop the symbol entirely if there are no internal callers).
- **(b) PUBLIC-API SURFACE** — re-exported by `src/index.ts`, OR returned
  as a public function's result type, OR consumed by the test-suite as
  documentation-of-contract. Action: **keep, add `@public` JSDoc tag**.
- **(c) UNCERTAIN** — needs maintainer judgment. Action: **flag in
  design doc, default to (b) treatment** (conservative).

Per Track-A recon (8 parallel searches over `src/`, `tests/`,
`package.json` `exports`), bucket counts are:

- (a) Genuinely internal — **13 symbols** confirmed safe to drop.
- (b) Public-API surface — **43+ symbols** confirmed via `src/index.ts`
  re-export or test consumption.
- (c) Uncertain — **2 symbols** (`resetEngineForTesting`,
  `CURVATURE_KIND_REGISTRY`). Default-to-(b) for both per this decision.

### Decision #2 — Large-file factor-outs are LOC-neutral on the public surface

Extractions are PURE refactors: they move LOC across files but do NOT
add or remove public exports. Every extracted function is `export`-less
(internal-only) unless an existing public consumer requires it. This
keeps Track-B fully orthogonal to Track-A.

Two specific factor-outs proceed (Track-B recon Proposal 1 + 2):

- **Proposal 1** — Extract the 10-arm curvature/GR-object dispatch out
  of `validator.ts`'s `infer()` switch into a registry pattern, mirroring
  the existing `CURVATURE_KIND_REGISTRY` in `curvature-composite.ts`.
  New module: `src/dimensional/validator-registry.ts` (~60–80 LOC).
- **Proposal 2** — Extract the `weyl-tensor` and `bianchi-residual` FD
  pipelines out of `lowering.ts`'s `lowerCurvature` dispatcher into the
  existing `src/numerical/curvature-lowering-helpers.ts` module.

**Proposal 3 (extracting covariant + tensor-partial-derivative arms) is
DEFERRED** — Track-B recon flagged it as medium-risk due to the
recursive `lowerNode` call inside `covariant-derivative`. Not blocked,
just not a v0.6.1 ROI. Carry to v0.7.0 if needed.

### Decision #3 — Dep-graph generator gets parser knowledge, not whitelist hacks

Three changes to `tools/create-dependency-graph/create-dependency-graph.ts`:

- **Parse `package.json` `exports`** — for every key in the `exports`
  field, mark the resolved file as implicitly reachable. Eliminates the
  `mathts-engine.ts` false-positive (and any future subpath additions).
- **Follow the `BRIDGE_EQUATIONS` static array** — `src/bridges/index.ts`
  declares the per-bridge equation modules as imports at file-top, but
  the generator may not be following the array's referenced symbols.
  Audit and fix. Eliminates the 40 `be-NN-*.ts` false-positives.
- **NO `.depgraphignore` file** — explicitly rejected. A regex whitelist
  is a hack; the goal is for the generator to be *correct*, not for it
  to ignore inconvenient findings. (This is the same principle that
  killed the C-9 "skip the bug" approach in the v0.6.0 doc-integrity
  review.)

If after both fixes any false-positives remain, document them by name
in the `unused-analysis.md` header with a one-line rationale.

### Decision #4 — `@public` JSDoc tag is the canonical surface marker

Per the v0.5.1 audit and the v0.6.0 doc-integrity review, the
public-API contract is currently implicit (re-exported from
`src/index.ts` ↔ public). Decision: every symbol re-exported by
`src/index.ts` gets a `@public` JSDoc tag at its declaration site.
This makes the contract explicit at the source of truth and lets the
dep-graph generator (or any future tool) classify exports without
parsing the manifest.

NOT in scope: a typescript-level `internal`/`public` separation via
distinct entry points. That's a v0.7.0+ concern.

### Decision #5 — Bench harnesses use the v0.5.0+ vitest-bench template

The three new bench files follow the existing convention (per
`bench/README.md` + `docs/architecture/benchmarks.md`): tinybench-backed,
inputs pre-built outside the bench callback (F4 pattern), sync benches
preferred, baseline numbers recorded in markdown after the run.

NO threshold gates. v0.6.0 set the precedent that bench gates are
informational only; v0.6.1 maintains this. A regression of >5%
wall-time on any of the three benches is a discussion item, not a
build failure.

### Decision #6 — CLAUDE.md staleness gets a single-pass refresh

Specific edits:

- Delete the `.ruff_cache` mention (cache no longer present).
- Update the `MathTSEngine` / `numerical/mathts-engine` paragraph if
  the dep-graph fix changes its classification.
- Re-tally the "8 established · 31 speculative · 3 highly-speculative ·
  0 invalid" bridge-status distribution against `src/bridges/index.ts`
  HEAD (per the v0.6.0 doc-integrity review's H-3 finding, this can
  drift between releases).

NOT in scope: a full CLAUDE.md rewrite. The file is the source-of-truth
session pointer; substantive structural change waits for v0.7.0.

### Decision #7 — BRIDGE-PHYSICS-AUDIT.md follow-ups are NOT v0.6.1 scope

The 42-bridge audit (commit `1c77a1e`) flagged ~19 contested framings
and several questionable `dimensional_signature` tags. v0.6.1 does NOT
re-encode any bridges. The audit's follow-ups remain in `todo.md` →
"From v0.6.0 doc-integrity review" → BRIDGE-PHYSICS-AUDIT.md
follow-ups. v0.6.1 leaves this section unchanged.

Rationale: bridge re-encoding requires per-bridge physics + literature
work, which is incompatible with a hygiene-pass tempo. Bundling it
here would either rush the physics or stretch the sprint past one
week.

### Decision #8 — `it.todo` markers are NOT v0.6.1 scope

The repo has 11 `it.todo` references; recon confirmed 9 are comment
references (documentation only) and 2 are real (the GL4 long-run gated
test + the AD-conformance describe.skip). Both real cases are
intentional. No-op for v0.6.1.

### Decision #9 — Adam+Eve adversarial review uses Opus subagents

Per the v0.5.1 carry-forward lesson ("Adam+Eve MCP availability is
fragile"), and the absence of `llm-gemini`/`llm-openai` MCPs in this
remote-execution environment, the design + plan vets use Opus
subagents as the v0.5.1-precedented fallback. Two parallel subagents
per artifact: one acting as "Adam" (steel-manning skeptic), one as
"Eve" (devil's-advocate red-team).

## Phase 2 — Large-file factor-outs

### Motivation

`lowering.ts` (1015 LOC) and `validator.ts` (816 LOC) are the two
largest src files outside the `bridges/index.ts` catalog (1774 LOC,
which is expected and not in scope). Track-B recon identified two
high-confidence extractions.

### Proposal 1 — `validator-registry.ts`

The `infer()` switch in `validator.ts` has 10 curvature/GR-object arms
(ricci/einstein/bianchi/riemann/weyl/kretschmann + killing-vector +
conserved-charge + stress-energy + cosmological-constant +
einstein-equation) that all follow the identical pattern:

```ts
case 'kind': {
  const r = validateKind(node, ...callbacks);
  if (entry.propagateFreeIndices) {
    for (const [label, counts] of r.freeIndices) ctx.freeIndices.set(label, counts);
  }
  return r.dim;
}
```

Three arms (conserved-charge, cosmological-constant, kretschmann-scalar)
have empty freeIndices and skip the loop; this is the only variation.

**Extraction:** create `src/dimensional/validator-registry.ts` with:

```ts
interface ValidatorEntry { validator: (node, ctx, infer) => ...; propagateFreeIndices: boolean; }
const VALIDATOR_REGISTRY: Record<NodeKind, ValidatorEntry> = { ... };
```

Then the `infer()` switch becomes 10 lines (one fall-through arm).

### Proposal 2 — `weyl` + `bianchi` FD pipelines into curvature-lowering-helpers

`lowerCurvature()` in `lowering.ts` has six arms; two of them (weyl
~85 LOC, bianchi ~42 LOC) are self-contained FD pipelines that already
have a natural home in `curvature-lowering-helpers.ts` (the v0.6.0 PD-6
extraction module).

**Extraction:** move the two arm bodies into the helpers module as
`lowerWeylTensor` + `lowerBianchiResidual`. The `lowerCurvature`
dispatcher becomes:

```ts
case 'weyl-tensor': return lowerWeylTensor(node, inputs, engine);
case 'bianchi-residual': return lowerBianchiResidual(node, inputs, engine);
```

### Pre-extraction snapshot tests

Both proposals require a TDD snapshot test BEFORE the extraction:

- **For Proposal 1:** snapshot the `validate()` result for each of the
  10 kinds on a representative fixture (Schwarzschild metric for the
  curvature kinds, perfect-fluid + cosmological-constant for the
  matter kinds, Killing-vector for the symmetry kinds). Diff before/after.
- **For Proposal 2:** snapshot the numerical Weyl tensor + Bianchi
  residual values on Schwarzschild at r=3·r_s (existing fixture). Diff
  before/after. Per Track-B recon, both are already covered by existing
  tests, but the snapshot makes the invariance explicit.

### Tests

- Existing `tests/dimensional/*` for the validator paths must all pass.
- Existing `tests/numerical/weyl-*` + `tests/dimensional/bianchi-*`
  must all pass.
- New snapshot test (lives in `tests/dimensional/validator-registry.test.ts`
  + reuses `tests/numerical/weyl-kerr-schild.test.ts` for the
  numerical side).

### Deliverable

- 1 commit for the snapshot test (RED-green-pinning).
- 1 commit for `validator-registry.ts` extraction.
- 1 commit for the weyl + bianchi helper moves.
- LOC budget: `validator.ts` ~816 → ~720 (drop ~90 LOC); `lowering.ts`
  ~1015 → ~890 (drop ~125 LOC).

## Adam+Eve adversarial review plan

Per Decision #9, two parallel Opus subagents per artifact:

- **Adam** — Steel-mans the design. Re-derives each claim from
  primary source (the recon outputs above are the immediate primary
  source). Flags any unsupported assertion.

Reviewers run AFTER this design doc lands. Findings consolidate into
`docs/planning/v0.6.1-Review-Findings.md`. Findings reconciled into
either the design (this doc) or the upcoming `v0.6.1-Implementation-Plan.md`.

## Risks

1. **Track A risk**: dropping `export` on a symbol that an unannounced
   downstream consumer depends on. Mitigation: every drop is on a
   non-`@public` symbol that does NOT appear in `tests/api/public-surface.test.ts`.
   The "balanced" policy (Decision #1) errs toward keeping any
   ambiguous symbol.
2. **Track B risk**: the validator-registry extraction introduces a
   subtle behavior change (e.g., the `propagateFreeIndices` flag is set
   wrong on one of the 10 kinds). Mitigation: the pre-extraction
   snapshot test (locked-in BEFORE the extraction) catches this.
3. **Track C risk**: the dep-graph generator fix over-broadens
   reachability and now hides real dead code. Mitigation: add a unit
   test fixture for the generator (a mini-source-tree) that pins both
   directions — known-reachable should be reachable; known-unused
   should still be flagged.
4. **Track D risk**: CLAUDE.md is the cross-session SoT; an edit that
   drifts from reality is high-impact. Mitigation: every edit is
   evidence-anchored (file existence check for `.ruff_cache`; grep
   count for bridge-status distribution).
5. **Track E risk**: the three benches reveal a real regression and
   force an unrelated investigation that blows the sprint timeline.
   Mitigation: Decision #5 commits to informational-only treatment.
   Any flagged regression becomes a follow-up `todo.md` item, not a
   v0.6.1 blocker.
